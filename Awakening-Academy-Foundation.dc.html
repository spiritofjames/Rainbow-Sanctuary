A gentle beginning

Four days of guided exploration.

Every activity is an invitation rather than a test. Children can ask questions, choose their pace, and take a pause at any time.

Day 1

The inner screen

Notice imagination, attention, and inner imagery through simple visualisation and whole-person awareness practices.

Day 2

Intuition and the living world

Explore curiosity, observation, and a felt sense of connection with nature and everyday life.

Day 3

Learning and creative intelligence

Use playful memory, attention, drawing, movement, and creative-expression practices to discover personal learning styles.

Day 4

Energy, boundaries, and integration

Practise naming inner experience, choosing grounding tools, and carrying a simple reflection into home life.

Before you decide

Questions families often ask.

Does my child need previous experience?

No. The Foundation is designed as an accessible first experience. We use age-aware language and do not expect children to perform or prove an ability.

What information do you need about my child?

Only the practical information needed to support participation safely. Please do not send diagnoses, medical or school records, government IDs, or unnecessary photos.

Is the Foundation a certification?

No. It is a four-day introduction and the gateway to Level I. A Certificate of Accomplishment comes only after the structured Academy pathway; it is not an academic, therapeutic, professional, or practitioner qualification.
