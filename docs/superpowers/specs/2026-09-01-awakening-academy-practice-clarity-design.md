# Awakening Academy Practice Clarity Update

## Purpose

Make the Academy’s public description more transparent for parents without turning spiritual practices into outcome promises. The Foundation remains the sole entry point for children ages 5–15.

## Content rules

- Name practices plainly: inner-screen work, short seated blindfold-perception exercises, intuitive object sensing, energy and boundary practices, focused intention, and—in the later advanced pathway—telekinesis and materialization as spiritual/experimental explorations.
- Describe what is observable separately from Rainbow Sanctuary’s spiritual interpretation. Individual experiences vary; no practice is a test, proof, public performance, clinical intervention, or scientific validation.
- Keep every child safe: voluntary participation, guardian support, brief stationary blindfold work only, no navigation or obstacle activity, and no pressure, ranking, or shame.
- Do not introduce schedules, pricing, payment, provider activation, child data collection, clinical claims, or guarantees.

## Page changes

1. **Children & Families** gives parents a concise, transparent overview of the kinds of practice and links into the Foundation journey.
2. **Foundation** explains the early practices and the exact blindfold boundary.
3. **Level I** explains discernment and intuitive object sensing in a structured, child-led setting.
4. **Level II** names focused intention, telekinesis, and materialization as invitation-only experimental spiritual work after the earlier pathway—not promised abilities.
5. **Awakening Academy roadmap** gives the full practice arc, Stephanie’s verified role, and direct answers to the questions parents will reasonably ask.

## Acceptance checks

- Pages preserve the parent-led Foundation CTA and child-certificate boundary.
- All named practices carry bounded claims and safeguarding context.
- Regression tests protect the practice, safety, and no-guarantee statements.
- Static validation, email checks, discovery generation, and tests pass locally.
