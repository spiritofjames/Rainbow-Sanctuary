# Awakening Academy Children’s Pathway Design

## Decision

Rainbow Sanctuary will present a parent-first, global children’s pathway for ages 5–15. The four-day Foundation Journey is both an accessible first experience and the prerequisite for Level 1. The default delivery is live online; invitation-based in-person cohorts may be offered through communities, retreats, and events.

## Journey

```text
Children & Families
  → Awakening Academy / Unlock the Potential
  → 4-Day Foundation Journey
  → parent review and readiness recommendation
  → Level 1
  → Level 2
  → Certificate of Accomplishment
  → ongoing Awakening Community
```

The Certificate of Accomplishment belongs to the child. It recognises completion of the structured journey and is not an academic, professional, therapeutic, or practitioner qualification. The separately existing Children’s Potential Coach Certification remains an adult pathway.

## Audience and safeguarding

- Public age range: 5–15.
- Ages 5–10 participate with a parent or guardian present or immediately available.
- Ages 11–15 require guardian consent and a reachable guardian; participation remains voluntary.
- Exercises are invitations, not tests. A child may pause or leave an exercise at any time.
- Public enquiry collection is minimal and parent-led. It does not request diagnoses, medical records, school records, government identification, or photos.
- The programme is an educational and experiential spiritual-wellbeing offering. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace qualified educational, medical, mental-health, or safeguarding support.

## Message hierarchy

1. Conventional education can be valuable while leaving less space for creativity, self-awareness, intuition, emotional language, and spiritual meaning.
2. The Academy offers a structured, age-aware space for those dimensions.
3. The Foundation is the simple first step; it lets a family experience the approach before considering a longer programme.
4. Progression is guided through a parent review and readiness recommendation.
5. The long-term destination is an ongoing community of practice, co-creation, and age-appropriate shared projects.

## Claims policy

Public copy may describe exploration, practice, participant experience, spiritual framework, and individual reflection. It may say a child explores attention, imagination, intuitive discernment, creative expression, emotional awareness, grounding, subtle-energy practices, and connection with nature.

It must not promise or state as verified fact psychic powers, out-of-body travel, photographic memory, illness recovery, diagnosis change, academic superiority, guaranteed abundance, removal of pollution through energy work, or measurable planetary healing. Use formulations such as “may notice,” “within Rainbow Sanctuary’s spiritual framework,” and “individual experiences vary.”

## Page changes

### `/children-and-family`

This is the parent trust page. It introduces whole-child conscious education, explains the six developmental pillars, distinguishes the child pathway from adult coach certification, publishes safeguarding boundaries, and routes to the Foundation Journey through one primary CTA.

### `/unlock-the-potential`

This becomes the master Academy roadmap. It defines the Foundation, guided parent review, Level 1, Level 2, Certificate of Accomplishment, and ongoing Awakening Community. Its primary CTA is the Foundation Journey.

### `/awakening-academy-foundation`

This is the dedicated four-day conversion page. It describes the four days, online-first format, occasional invitation-based in-person cohorts, age and guardian requirements, what to prepare, and the parent-led enquiry route. No dates, fees, capacity, payment links, Zoom links, or live email automation are invented or activated before separate operational approval.

### Existing adult coach certification

The child pages explicitly identify it as professional adult training and do not present it as the child’s certificate.

## Foundation content

1. **The inner screen:** attention, visualisation, reflection, and introductory blindfolded-perception exercises.
2. **Intuition and the living world:** intuitive recognition, relationships with plants, animals, food, objects, and nature; respect and discernment.
3. **Learning and creative intelligence:** memory exercises, creative expression, intuitive reading practices, painting, poetry, movement, and personal gifts.
4. **Energy, boundaries, and integration:** grounding, cleansing and protection practices, etheric-hand awareness within the spiritual framework, daily-life integration, and next steps.

## Stephanie positioning

Stephanie Wu is positioned as the founder of Rainbow Sanctuary and creator of the Awakening Academy pathway. Copy describes her work as bringing together meditation, intuitive development, creative expression, subtle-energy practices, nature connection, and what Rainbow Sanctuary understands as higher-dimensional consciousness. Her role is to create a structured field for children to explore, practise, question, and develop discernment, not to promise a predetermined perception or ability.

## Enquiry and operations boundaries

The Foundation CTA uses the existing parent-led `/apply` intake, with `reason=family` and `program=awakening-academy-foundation`. The HubSpot taxonomy recognises this programme. Staging uses ordinary safely handled enquiries only. It does not create production payments, calendar events, Zoom links, real participant messages, or provider configuration.

## Acceptance criteria

- A parent can understand the full journey in under two minutes.
- The Foundation is visually and semantically the first required step.
- Adult coach certification and child completion recognition are unmistakably separate.
- Every child-facing claim complies with the claims policy.
- Primary CTAs work after the design runtime renders, on mobile and desktop.
- Routes, metadata, sitemap, llms files, and canonical discovery are consistent.
- Existing forms retain their protected intake behaviour.
- Full validation passes and the work is promoted only to protected staging after a clean merge check.
