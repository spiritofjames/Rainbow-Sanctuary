What children explore

Practice, reflection, and choice.

Rainbow Sanctuary uses spiritual language for subjective experience. These practices are invitations for attention and reflection—not medical services, diagnostic tests, or guaranteed outcomes.

Inner screen and blindfold perception

Brief, stationary, voluntary activities with imagery, colours, or forms; a child can remove the eye mask at any time.

Intuitive sensing

Noticing impressions around everyday objects, nature, feelings, and creative work while distinguishing observation, imagination, and uncertainty.

Energy and boundaries

Grounding, personal space, rest, and care are practised within Rainbow Sanctuary’s spiritual framework.

Focused intention

Later, eligible children may be invited to advanced experimental practices; these are always optional and never a measure of worth.

Family-centred and safeguard-aware

Guardians remain part of the circle of care.

Ages 5–10A parent or guardian is present or immediately available.
Ages 11–15A guardian consents and stays reachable during live sessions.
Every ageParticipation is voluntary; no blindfold navigation, obstacles, extended darkness, or pressure to continue.

Rainbow Sanctuary’s children’s work is educational and experiential spiritual-wellbeing support. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace qualified medical, mental-health, or educational care.

A distinct adult pathway

Are you an adult who wants to work with children?

The Children’s Potential Coach Certification is professional adult training in child-centred session design, family communication, safeguarding, ethics, scope, and reflective practice. It is separate from the child’s Academy route and Certificate of Accomplishment.
