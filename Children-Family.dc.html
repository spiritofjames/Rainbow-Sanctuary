Not a performance standard

Each child meets the work in their own way.

We do not ask children to prove an ability. The work makes room for curiosity, questions, rest, and personal pace.

What we cultivate

Six foundations for an awake, grounded life.

Inner awareness

Listening to attention, sensation, imagination, and emotion with curiosity.

Intuitive discernment

Learning to ask what is observed, imagined, felt, known, and still uncertain.

Creative expression

Giving inner experience a voice through art, movement, play, story, and reflection.

Emotional coherence

Finding age-appropriate language, grounding, and respectful ways to respond.

Energetic boundaries

Practising choice, personal space, rest, and returning to the body.

Living connection

Growing care for relationships, place, and the living world through practical responsibility.

Family-centred and safeguard-aware

Guardians remain part of the circle of care.

Ages 5–10A parent or guardian is present or immediately available.
Ages 11–15A guardian consents and stays reachable during live sessions.
Every ageParticipation is voluntary; a child may pause or decline any activity.

Rainbow Sanctuary’s children’s work is educational and experiential spiritual-wellbeing support. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace qualified medical, mental-health, or educational care.

A distinct adult pathway

Are you an adult who wants to work with children?

The Children’s Potential Coach Certification is professional adult training in child-centred session design, family communication, safeguarding, ethics, scope, and reflective practice. It is separate from the child’s Academy route and Certificate of Accomplishment.
