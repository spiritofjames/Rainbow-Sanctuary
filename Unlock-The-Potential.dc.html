Practice in plain language

What may be explored—and how we hold it.

Rainbow Sanctuary’s spiritual framework includes inner-screen work, brief seated blindfold-perception activities, intuitive object sensing, energy and boundary practices, and focused intention. In the later pathway, invitation-only experimental explorations may include telekinesis and materialization.

These are personal and spiritual explorations. They are not clinical services, public tests, evidence of a child’s worth, promised abilities, or scientific validation.

What is observable

A child may describe images, impressions, feelings, questions, or creative work. The group distinguishes observation, imagination, interpretation, and uncertainty.

What remains optional

A child can pause, decline, or simply observe. Blindfold activities are brief and stationary: never navigation, obstacle work, extended darkness, or a challenge to complete.

Important clarity

What the Academy is—and is not.

Does every child begin with the Foundation?

Yes. The 4-Day Foundation is the shared entry point for children new to the Academy. It lets the child and family experience the approach before a longer commitment.

Are telekinesis or materialization guaranteed?

No. In the Academy these words name later, invitation-only spiritual and experimental explorations. No child is expected to demonstrate an outcome, and they are not presented as scientific validation.

What does the Certificate of Accomplishment mean?

It recognises a child’s participation and learning within the Academy’s structured pathway. It is not an academic, therapeutic, professional, or practitioner qualification.

Is this a health or clinical service?

No. This is educational and experiential spiritual-wellbeing work. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace qualified health, mental-health, or educational support.

Rainbow Sanctuary does not promise particular intuitive abilities, health outcomes, academic results, or life outcomes. Children’s experiences vary, and guardians remain essential partners in the process.
