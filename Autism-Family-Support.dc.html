Our exploratory framework

Different nervous systems deserve room to be understood.

We see autism as a diverse way of experiencing and organising the world—not a person to be fixed. Our working framework explores how gentle rhythm, sensory comfort, creativity, predictable structure, and respectful relationship may help a person and their family feel more supported.

These are principles we are actively learning from, not final answers or promises of a particular outcome. Every person has different needs, strengths, preferences, and sources of support.

Our principles
  • Choice before compliance
  • Strengths alongside challenges
  • Predictability without pressure
  • Family wisdom and informed consent
What this does not claim: This is a non-clinical wellbeing offering. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or promise neurological changes, and it complements—not replaces—appropriate healthcare, education, therapeutic support, or crisis care.

How it works

Participation is free. Support is optional.

1. Register

A parent or guardian shares the participant’s photo, name, age, and country. A completed form is the registration.

2. Prepare

We confirm the registration by email. There is no Zoom session or requirement to attend live; the weekly practice is held at Tuesday 23:00 Beijing time.

3. Rest

At the corresponding local time, make space for rest and familiarity. Optional contributions help keep this free pathway open.

Important: This is not emergency support. For immediate safety concerns, seek appropriate local emergency or crisis support.
