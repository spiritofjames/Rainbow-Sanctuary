Why support matters

We want meaningful experiences of care, reflection, and connection to remain available beyond a person's immediate financial circumstances. Voluntary support from the wider community helps us hold that intention responsibly and sustainably.

What your contribution supports

Small acts of support can create wider access.

01

Free and accessible pathways

Support helps us offer selected group sessions and community experiences without requiring every participant to pay.

02

People and families in need

It gives the team more room to welcome people whose present circumstances would otherwise prevent participation.

03

Care that can continue

It contributes to the practical work behind accessible sessions: coordination, communication, facilitation, and safe digital infrastructure.

An open invitation
