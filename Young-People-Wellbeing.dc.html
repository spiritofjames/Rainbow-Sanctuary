A careful first step

Supportive, not clinical.

1. Request review

Share only the minimum context needed for the team to assess fit. Do not send records, diagnoses, or emergency information.

3. Prepare privately

Approved participants receive practical preparation and reminder messages; the initial format does not require Zoom attendance.

Urgent support: This is not a crisis or emergency service and does not replace licensed mental-health care. If someone may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency or crisis support now.
