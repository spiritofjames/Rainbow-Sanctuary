## Start with questions, not a booking assumption

Awakening Your Inner Light is advertised for **18–24 January 2027**, a seven-day, six-night retreat with an investment of **USD 3,500 per person**. Registering interest starts a private conversation; it does not reserve a place, confirm suitability or create a payment obligation. This guide is for adults considering an enquiry and wanting to understand the practical next steps.

**In brief:** use the current retreat page for published details, request clarification before deciding, and wait for written acceptance and confirmed arrangements before booking travel. This guide is not medical advice or a determination that plant-medicine participation is suitable for any particular person.

## Confirm which event you are reading about

The active page is [Awakening Your Inner Light, January 2027](/awakening-your-inner-light-Jan-2027). Photographs, videos and accounts of the completed October 2026 gathering help show a past experience, but they do not establish the January itinerary, venue or what another person will experience. The page labels documentary and illustrative material for that reason.

When discussing the event, use its full name and dates. If you have an older link, saved image or forwarded message, check it against the current page and ask about any difference. A beautiful preview is an invitation to learn more, not a replacement for the confirmed programme and practical terms relevant to your decision.

## Ask what the price includes

The published investment is USD 3,500 per person. The current page says the final schedule, accommodation arrangements, meal plan, specific practices and follow-up details are confirmed in writing before payment. Ask what is included, what is optional, whether there are additional costs, and how the payment schedule and cancellation terms work.

Do not infer inclusions from a past gathering or assume an activity pictured is guaranteed. Local tours, nature exploration and optional classes may have separate availability or costs. Use only the authorized payment route supplied through the appropriate process. An enquiry or discovery-call request is not itself an instruction to pay for the retreat.

## Understand the suitability conversation

The retreat is described as spiritual and experiential, not as medical treatment or psychotherapy. Plant-medicine experiences can be intense and unpredictable, and no particular outcome is promised. The relevant question is not whether someone is spiritually ready in an abstract sense, but whether the actual format and risks have been explained and suitability has been considered through the appropriate private process.

Ask how that process works and who is responsible for it. Relevant health and medication questions belong in confidential screening with appropriate qualified input; an open website article cannot assess them. Do not change prescribed medication to attend without guidance from your prescribing clinician. Continue appropriate professional care, and do not treat participation as a substitute for that care. For independent background on unpredictability, possible adverse effects and medication interactions, see the [US National Institutes of Health overview of psychedelic and dissociative drugs](https://health.nih.gov/health-topics-a-z/psychedelic-drugs).

## Ask about choice and support

Before accepting a place, understand what is essential, what is optional, what alternatives exist and how someone can pause or decline a practice. Ask about the support available during the week and how concerns are raised. A general reassurance is less useful than a clear explanation of the actual arrangements and limits.

You can also ask about preparation and integration. What information arrives before the retreat? What sessions are planned during the week? Is any support after the retreat included, and what is its scope or separate cost? The [integration reflection guide](/knowledge/turning-insight-into-daily-practice) discusses everyday practice, but it does not replace the retreat's specific preparation or follow-up instructions.

## A discovery call is arranged with the team

The website offers a way to request a discovery call. That means the team contacts you to arrange a suitable time; it is not an instant calendar booking. You can use the conversation to ask practical questions and explain what you need to understand before deciding. First-time ceremonial experience is not assumed, and uncertainty is a valid reason to slow down.

Keep the initial enquiry focused and avoid unnecessary medical records, identity documents or other people's private information. The team should explain the appropriate route for any further confidential screening. If you do not receive the expected acknowledgement, check your spam folder or contact the team rather than assuming that a place or call has been booked.

## A checklist before committing

Bring these questions to your conversation:

- Are the dates and final venue confirmed for the invitation I am considering?
- What is the daily programme, and which activities are optional?
- What does USD 3,500 include, and what costs are separate?
- What are the written payment and cancellation terms?
- How is confidential suitability screening handled?
- What preparation, in-person support and integration guidance are provided?
- How do I pause, decline or raise a concern?
- What written acceptance and travel instructions should I wait for?

Do not book flights solely because you have submitted an enquiry. The published page directs prospective participants to wait for written acceptance, final venue confirmation, payment and cancellation terms, legal-readiness confirmation and explicit travel instructions. If any of those remain unclear, ask before making a commitment that depends on them.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does registering interest reserve a retreat place?

No. It begins a team follow-up and fit conversation, not a reservation or automatic acceptance. A discovery-call request likewise asks the team to arrange a conversation. Wait for the required private review and written confirmation before treating attendance as agreed or making arrangements that assume you have a place.

### Is the January programme the same as October's?

Past-gathering material provides context, not a fixed January itinerary. The current page says the January daily programme and practical arrangements are being finalized and will be supplied before commitment. Ask about the actual January invitation, including practices, accommodation, inclusions and follow-up, rather than inferring them from photographs or testimony.

### Can I enquire if I am unsure about attending?

Yes. The page explicitly invites questions and a private conversation before deciding. You do not need to commit in order to ask about format, risks, support or practical arrangements. Choosing not to proceed after receiving information is a valid outcome of a considered enquiry, not a failure to participate.
