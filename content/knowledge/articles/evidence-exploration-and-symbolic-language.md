## Why does the library use different kinds of language?

Rainbow Sanctuary brings together practical community experience, ecological care, reflective writing, and spiritual or philosophical exploration. These do not all carry the same kind of support. Treating them as if they do would be confusing and, in some cases, unsafe.

Our public material therefore aims to make the difference visible rather than hiding it behind confident language.

## What counts as established knowledge?

Established knowledge is information supported by credible evidence, relevant expertise, and appropriate real-world testing. When a topic affects health, safety, law, money, or rights, established standards and qualified professionals matter. The Knowledge Library does not replace those sources of care or advice.

## What does exploration mean here?

Exploration means a question, hypothesis, emerging field, or personal line of inquiry that has not been established as fact. Some source texts imagine future relationships between consciousness, technology, and the cosmos. Those ideas are presented as authorial exploration. They are not technical specifications, scientific findings, or promises about what a person will experience.

Readers are encouraged to stay curious and discerning: ask what is known, what is proposed, what evidence would be needed, and what remains a matter of personal meaning.

## What is symbolic or spiritual language?

Symbolic language can help people speak about meaning, belonging, imagination, and inner experience. Its value does not depend on proving it as a literal scientific account. It should never be used to pressure someone, dismiss their concerns, or make claims about diagnosis, treatment, or guaranteed transformation.

## How should I use this library?

Use it as a place to orient, reflect, and formulate better questions. Keep your judgment active. Where a decision has serious consequences, seek the relevant professional, legal, medical, or local expertise.
