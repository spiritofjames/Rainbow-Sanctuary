Why maintenance

Give the practice time to become familiar.

Within the Rainbow Sanctuary approach, the completed ReGeneration work is held through a twelve-layer, 144-point framework. Maintenance is the continuing space to return to that framework after the intensive programme has ended.

It is not a separate programme to rush through. It is a regular rhythm for noticing what supports your own sense of steadiness, refining the conditions around your practice, and allowing the work to settle gradually into everyday life.

Initial Maintenance · weekly cycles

A foundation for continuing practice.

Initial Maintenance has a 4-Monday or 12-Monday format. The completed 12-Monday cycle ran from 17 August through 2 November 2026. Its three-part rhythm is shown below; new opening dates are not currently published.

Month 01

Settle the new rhythm

Make room for the practice in a way that feels sustainable. Notice the routines, environments, and inner patterns that help you return to a more settled pace.

Month 02

Build continuity

Continue with gentle consistency. This phase invites a closer relationship with rest, attention, and the daily choices that support your own experience of the work.

Month 03

Integrate into daily life

Let the rhythm become more familiar. The focus is on carrying forward what feels useful, so the practice can remain present beyond the opening cycle.

Questions & answers

Clear details before you continue.

Who can register for Maintenance?+

Maintenance is for people who have completed both ReGeneration Level I and ReGeneration Level II.

Do I need to join live?+

No. This is held remotely. There is no Zoom room, call, check-in, or live attendance required from participants.

What is the timing of the opening cycle?+

The completed opening cycle began on 17 August 2026 and ended on 2 November 2026. New initial-cycle dates are not currently published. Contact our team to discuss the next opening.

Is this an automatic subscription?+

The initial 4-Monday and 12-Monday cycles were one-time commitments. Ongoing Maintenance is a separate USD 110 monthly subscription for two remote sessions each month. Completing an initial cycle does not automatically subscribe you.
