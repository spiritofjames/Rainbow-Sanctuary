Why maintenance

Give the practice time to become familiar.

Within the Rainbow Sanctuary approach, the completed ReGeneration work is held through a twelve-layer, 144-point framework. Maintenance is the continuing space to return to that framework after the intensive programme has ended.

It is not a separate programme to rush through. It is a regular rhythm for noticing what supports your own sense of steadiness, refining the conditions around your practice, and allowing the work to settle gradually into everyday life.

First three months · weekly

A three-part opening cycle.

The opening phase is held every Monday from 17 August through 2 November 2026. Each month has a distinct emphasis while preserving one calm, consistent rhythm.

Month 01

Settle the new rhythm

Make room for the practice in a way that feels sustainable. Notice the routines, environments, and inner patterns that help you return to a more settled pace.

Month 02

Build continuity

Continue with gentle consistency. This phase invites a closer relationship with rest, attention, and the daily choices that support your own experience of the work.

Month 03

Integrate into daily life

Let the rhythm become more familiar. The focus is on carrying forward what feels useful, so the practice can remain present beyond the opening cycle.

After the opening cycle

From weekly rhythm to twice-monthly care.

After the first three months, the Maintenance rhythm moves to twice monthly. Future dates will be shared with registered participants before the next cycle opens.

Everyone's relationship with a long-term practice is personal. The programme invites you to stay attentive to intention, rest, food, movement, and the rhythms of daily life in the way that is right for you.
