Level I themes

From curiosity to a steady, shared practice.

Children develop language for what they notice, while guardians remain part of a practical, age-appropriate support circle.

Inner-screen practice

Notice attention, imagination, sensations, and inner imagery without needing to judge or fix them.

Discernment

Practise asking grounded questions about what is felt, imagined, observed, and still unknown.

Intuitive object sensing

Explore simple psychometry: holding a safe everyday object and sharing impressions as personal observations, not facts about another person.

Creative expression

Use art, reflection, movement, and story to make inner experience shareable in a child-led way.

Living-world connection

Explore wonder, care, and close observation in relationship with nature and everyday life.

Grounded energetic boundaries

Learn simple practices for choice, rest, personal space, and returning to the body.

Family integration

Bring simple language and optional home practices into daily family life.

Progression is personal

Completion of the Foundation does not automatically enrol a child in Level I.

After the Foundation, a guardian conversation and Stephanie or the team’s readiness recommendation help determine whether Level I, a later cohort, or another form of support is the right next step. Structured observation is never a public test, ranking, or promise of a particular result.

The Academy is educational and experiential spiritual-wellbeing work. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace qualified health, mental-health, or educational care.
