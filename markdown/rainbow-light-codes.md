A creative meditation practice using color, breath, visualization, and reflective attention to explore your inner atmosphere and support everyday self-regulation.

The learning journey

## Structured enough to follow. Spacious enough to be personal.

Each part builds from observation into practice, then brings the learning back into everyday life.

**01 Color as a reflective language**

Explore personal associations with color rather than assigning fixed universal meanings.

**02 The light-code sequence**

Learn a guided breath and visualization structure you can adapt to your needs.

**03 Tracking your response**

Notice sensations, emotions, images, and after-effects with curiosity.

**04 Daily integration**

Create brief practices for transitions, creative work, rest, and practitioner self-care.

Stephanie’s living-systems framework

## Information becomes practice. Practice meets reality.

The work moves through three connected lenses. None stands alone, and each remains open to reflection, evidence, and your own experience.

01 · Information **What story or pattern is operating?**

02 · Energy **How is it felt, held, or expressed?**

03 · Reality **What grounded action can change?**
