# Begin with context, *not a calendar.*

Tell us what brings you here. Private-healing requests are reviewed before scheduling; other enquiries are routed to the most relevant program or event.

01 **Share** Your context and intention

02 **Review** Scope, consent, and fit

03 **Schedule** Private link if accepted

04 **Integrate** Written follow-up where applicable

Before you submit

## A request is not an acceptance.

We may suggest a different Rainbow Sanctuary pathway, ask for clarification, or recommend qualified care outside our scope.

Do not submit medical records, diagnoses, government identifiers, emergency information, or unnecessary details about another person.
