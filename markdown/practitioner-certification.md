## Practice standards are part of the curriculum.

### Consent

Clear agreements and respect for each participant’s agency.

### Scope

Honest language about what the role can and cannot provide.

### Referral

Knowing when another professional or specialist is needed.

### Reflection

Feedback, supervision, self-awareness, and continuing development.

## Confirm the credential before you commit.

Program duration, assessment, practicum, certification title, recognition, and any professional eligibility are still final inputs. A consultation will distinguish what is confirmed from what is proposed.
