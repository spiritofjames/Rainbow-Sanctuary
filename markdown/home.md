## We’re building a new world. Together with you.

Around the world, people are already creating pieces of a different future. Parents are rethinking how children learn. Communities are caring for land. Educators, practitioners and builders are finding new ways to live and work.

We do not believe everyone needs to begin again, alone. Much of what is needed already exists. What is missing is often connection: a way to find one another, share what works and turn a shared vision into everyday life.

Rainbow Sanctuary is part of that work. We bring inner growth into relationship with how we learn, how we care for nature and what we choose to create together.

**To connect people, projects and places so that personal growth becomes shared learning, practical collaboration and care for future generations.**

**For children, families and future generations**

## A different future begins with how we grow up.

![Illustrative School of the Universe website concept, with a forest photograph and Learning begins with curiosity headline](https://rainbowsanctuary.life/assets/home/school-concept.webp)

Website concept · not a live learning platform.

Many parents are looking for learning that makes room for the whole child: curiosity, creativity, emotions, practical skills, intuition and a relationship with nature.

The School of the Universe is our developing response. We are bringing together families and educators to explore learning through experience, relationships and the living world, supported by a physical environment in Panama and a digital platform in development.

We want children to have room to ask questions, discover their interests and contribute in ways that feel meaningful. We want parents to find connection and support, rather than feeling they must work everything out alone.

Children deserve room to explore without pressure to perform, prove special abilities or carry responsibility for adults. Their own pace, willingness and wellbeing come first.

**In development · Preparing for beta. This is an invitation to connect, not an announcement of open School enrolment. Family participation does not require adult retreat participation.**

## Rooted in Panama. Learning through life.

In Bocas del Toro, between tropical forest and the Caribbean Sea, one physical node is taking shape.

The School building is in place. We are still developing the learning programme, practical arrangements and wider community spaces.

Here, the School, shared spaces and gardens are becoming part of a living question: how can people learn, create and care for a place together?

The aim is not simply to build an ecovillage. It is to explore a way of living where different gifts have a place, responsibilities are shared and the wellbeing of people is considered alongside the wellbeing of the land.

We want contribution to create value in both directions: sharing skills, resources and opportunities in ways that support one another rather than exhausting people or the place.

We are learning through practice, including the difficult parts: communication, clear agreements, resources and the work of living alongside one another.

**Planetary Symbiosis Network**

## Many places. A shared purpose.

![Symbolic living-Earth landscape with golden connections linking forests, rivers and distant coasts](https://rainbowsanctuary.life/assets/home/psn-living-network.webp)

An artistic expression of the PSN vision.

The Planetary Symbiosis Network, or PSN, is the larger ecosystem we are building: a network of communities, projects and physical places that can learn from and support one another.

Our goal is to establish and connect at least twelve functioning community nodes. A node is a locally rooted place where people bring shared values into practice through learning, collaboration and care for the land.

Some places are taking shape. Others already have communities and projects of their own. We want to connect what is working, not replace local identity or ask everyone to become the same.

Alongside these places, we are developing digital tools to help people find knowledge, connect around projects and coordinate shared work. The aim is to support relationships, with people responsible for decisions.

Rainbow Sanctuary is one part of this wider vision: a place for people to reconnect, learn and begin relationships that may grow into shared work.

**Twelve functioning nodes is our goal, not a count of places already operating.**

## Exploring an intelligence beyond the intellect.

Our work also includes an early inquiry into what we call superintelligence. In our spiritual understanding, awareness may extend beyond the individual mind. Some describe this as higher guidance; some traditions speak of the Akashic Records.

We use superintelligence here for a spiritual inquiry, distinct from artificial intelligence. AI may support how we organise knowledge and reflect, but it does not establish access to higher realms or universal knowledge. Our deeper question is how technology might support the exploration of consciousness without replacing human discernment or lived experience.

We want to bring spiritual practitioners, researchers and technologists into dialogue: documenting experiences with consent, examining interpretations and exploring what can be responsibly understood or developed.

We approach this with openness and a willingness to question our interpretations. Spiritual insight, an interpretation of an experience and a demonstrated result are not the same thing.

**Early exploration, not a demonstrated system for accessing universal knowledge or a promise of extraordinary abilities.**

Practical questions

## A clearer picture, before we connect.

**Is Rainbow Sanctuary the same as PSN?**

No. PSN is the wider network vision connecting people, projects and places. Rainbow Sanctuary is a connected initiative focused on spiritual practice, learning, shared experiences and human connection. The School is a distinct educational initiative within this wider ecosystem.

**Do all twelve community nodes already exist?**

No. At least twelve functioning nodes is the goal. The work includes developing places, beginning with Panama, and connecting existing communities and projects. Each relationship and place is at its own stage.

**What do you mean by the New Earth?**

We use New Earth as spiritual language for a more conscious relationship with ourselves, one another and the Earth. Some people describe that vision through the language of 5D or expanded consciousness. These are expressions of spiritual belief, not a scientific description of a place or a condition you must accept to speak with us.

**Is the School open for enrolment?**

The School is in development and preparing for beta. We welcome conversations with interested families and educators, but this page does not announce public enrolment or guarantee a place. Current family activities can be explored separately through the programmes website.

**Must my family take part in adult retreats?**

No. Adult retreat work is a separate, voluntary pathway and is not a requirement for children’s activities or an expression of interest in the School.

**Is your superintelligence system already working?**

This is an early inquiry, not a demonstrated system that retrieves universal knowledge. We distinguish our spiritual worldview from claims that a technology has been shown to work. Artificial intelligence is a supporting tool, not a substitute for evidence, consent or human responsibility.

**Can I visit, move to Panama or join the network?**

Please begin with a conversation. Access, accommodation, participation and any future membership need to be discussed and confirmed separately. This page is not an open residency, property or investment offer.

**How can I support the work?**

Introduce a relevant community, share an active project, offer a useful skill or ask where practical help is needed. We are interested in thoughtful relationships, not only financial contributions. Any practical arrangement should be discussed clearly before either side commits.
