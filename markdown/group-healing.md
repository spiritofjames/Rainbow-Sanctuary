Questions & answers

## Helpful clarity before you choose.

Every pathway is different. These answers can help you find the most suitable starting point.

**01 How do I choose between the four pathways?**

Choose Online Group Healing for a guided shared session; Regeneration Maintenance only if you have already completed ReGeneration Levels I and II; Autism & Family Support or Young People’s Wellbeing Support for their dedicated registration pathways.

**02 Can I attend Online Group Healing without joining a longer programme?**

Yes. It is a standalone shared session. You can choose a published date and complete its direct registration without committing to a longer pathway.

**03 What happens after I register?**

Your confirmation explains the next practical step for the pathway you selected. Paid bookings receive their payment confirmation; free registrations receive the information relevant to the scheduled practice.

**04 What if I am not sure which group pathway is right?**

You can send the team a question before registering. Share only the context you are comfortable providing, and the team can help you identify the most appropriate next step.
