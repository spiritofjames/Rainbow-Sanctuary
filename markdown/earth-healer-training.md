A service-oriented training in ecological awareness, elemental relationship, group facilitation, and responsible participation in collective wellbeing.

The learning journey

## Structured enough to follow. Spacious enough to be personal.

Each part builds from observation into practice, then brings the learning back into everyday life.

**01 Relationship with place**

Develop observation, gratitude, reciprocity, and a grounded practice of listening to land and community.

**02 The elemental lens**

Use earth, water, air, fire, and space as reflective frameworks—not substitutes for environmental science.

**03 Collective field awareness**

Study group dynamics, facilitation agreements, consent, scope, and referral.

**04 Service design**

Shape a small, responsible project aligned with the Planetary Symbiosis Network vision.

Stephanie’s living-systems framework

## Information becomes practice. Practice meets reality.

The work moves through three connected lenses. None stands alone, and each remains open to reflection, evidence, and your own experience.

01 · Information **What story or pattern is operating?**

02 · Energy **How is it felt, held, or expressed?**

03 · Reality **What grounded action can change?**

Possible takeaways

## Change is practised, not promised.

What you take away depends on your context, participation, support system, and the time you give integration.

**Ecological attention** Deepen how you notice and relate to living systems.

**Facilitation foundations** Create clearer agreements for group and place-based practice.

**Responsible scope** Know the limits of your role and when specialist expertise is needed.

**A service project** Define a tangible contribution rooted in reciprocity.
