# Intuitive Perception *Sensitivity with discernment*

A grounded training for noticing subtle impressions, testing interpretation, and working with sensitivity in a way that respects evidence, consent, and personal boundaries.

Start with your lived experience

## This may meet you where you are.

You do not need to arrive with the right language or a perfected version of yourself. These are common starting points—not promises or diagnoses.

### A familiar starting point

You feel highly sensitive but sometimes overwhelmed or unsure.

### A pattern worth exploring

You want to separate intuition from anxiety or projection.

### A readiness to practise

You support others and need stronger energetic and ethical boundaries.

### A grounding intention

You will test impressions against context, evidence, and feedback.

The learning journey

## Structured enough to follow. Spacious enough to be personal.

Each part builds from observation into practice, then brings the learning back into everyday life.

**01 Grounding before perception**

Establish calm, sensory orientation, and an observable baseline.

**02 Channels of noticing**

Explore images, sensations, words, emotions, and pattern recognition without assuming certainty.

**03 Discernment practice**

Compare impressions with context, evidence, bias, and feedback.

**04 Consent and communication**

Learn when not to share an impression and how to phrase uncertainty responsibly.

Stephanie’s living-systems framework

## Information becomes practice. Practice meets reality.

The work moves through three connected lenses. None stands alone, and each remains open to reflection, evidence, and your own experience.

01 · Information **What story or pattern is operating?**

02 · Energy **How is it felt, held, or expressed?**

03 · Reality **What grounded action can change?**

Possible takeaways

## Change is practised, not promised.

What you take away depends on your context, participation, support system, and the time you give integration.

**More steadiness** Stay connected to the present while noticing subtle information.

**Better discernment** Treat impressions as information to explore, not automatic truth.

**Stronger boundaries** Reduce over-responsibility for other people’s experience.

**Ethical language** Communicate sensitivity with consent and humility.
