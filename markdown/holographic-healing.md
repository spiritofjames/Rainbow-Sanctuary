The learning journey

## Structured enough to follow. Spacious enough to be personal.

Each part builds from observation into practice, then brings the learning back into everyday life.

**01 The whole-person map**

Look at physical, emotional, relational, environmental, and meaning-making layers together.

**02 Patterns and context**

Track when experiences appear, what surrounds them, and what helps—without self-diagnosis.

**03 Everyday practices**

Explore breath, rest, attention, journaling, supportive routines, and clearer communication.

**04 Care pathways**

Understand scope, red flags, and when to seek medical or mental-health support.

Stephanie’s living-systems framework

## Information becomes practice. Practice meets reality.

The work moves through three connected lenses. None stands alone, and each remains open to reflection, evidence, and your own experience.

01 · Information **What story or pattern is operating?**

02 · Energy **How is it felt, held, or expressed?**

03 · Reality **What grounded action can change?**

Possible takeaways

## Change is practised, not promised.

What you take away depends on your context, participation, support system, and the time you give integration.

**A wider view** See wellbeing as a set of interacting conditions.

**Useful observations** Bring clearer patterns and questions to appropriate professionals.

**Family language** Discuss needs and routines with less blame.

**Responsible self-care** Use gentle practices without replacing needed treatment.
