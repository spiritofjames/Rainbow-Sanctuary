# Children’s Potential Coach *A responsible pathway into child & family facilitation*

Training for adults who want to facilitate strengths-based learning experiences for children, communicate well with families, and work within clear ethical and professional boundaries.

[Discuss the certification pathway](https://rainbowsanctuary.life/apply?reason=certification&program=childrens-potential-coach-certification) USD 7,710 package

Start with your lived experience

## This may meet you where you are.

You do not need to arrive with the right language or a perfected version of yourself. These are common starting points—not promises or diagnoses.

### A familiar starting point

You are an educator, parent, facilitator, or practitioner drawn to children’s development.

### A pattern worth exploring

You want structure—not just activities—for holding child-centered spaces.

### A readiness to practise

You are willing to study consent, safeguarding, scope, and referral.

### A grounding intention

You welcome supervision, feedback, and continuing development.

The learning journey

## Structured enough to follow. Spacious enough to be personal.

Each part builds from observation into practice, then brings the learning back into everyday life.

**01 The child-centered lens**

Explore development, temperament, strengths, learning differences, and the importance of avoiding labels.

**02 Facilitation design**

Build age-aware sessions using play, creativity, attention, movement, and reflection.

**03 Working with families**

Practise consultation, expectation-setting, progress conversations, confidentiality, and boundaries.

**04 Ethics and practicum**

Apply safeguarding, scope, referral, supervision, and reflective practice in a reviewed learning process.

Stephanie’s living-systems framework

## Information becomes practice. Practice meets reality.

The work moves through three connected lenses. None stands alone, and each remains open to reflection, evidence, and your own experience.

01 · Information **What story or pattern is operating?**

02 · Energy **How is it felt, held, or expressed?**

03 · Reality **What grounded action can change?**

Possible takeaways

## Change is practised, not promised.

What you take away depends on your context, participation, support system, and the time you give integration.

**A facilitation framework** Plan purposeful, age-aware child sessions.

**Family communication** Hold clearer conversations with caregivers.

**Ethical scope** Know what the role includes—and what it does not.

**A development plan** Identify practicum, feedback, and continuing-learning needs.
