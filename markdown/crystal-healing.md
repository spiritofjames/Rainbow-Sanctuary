The learning journey

## Structured enough to follow. Spacious enough to be personal.

Each part builds from observation into practice, then brings the learning back into everyday life.

**01 Selection and care**

Explore sourcing, cleaning, storage, and the personal associations you bring to different stones.

**02 Intention and attention**

Use crystals as prompts for focus, reflection, and ritual rather than medical treatment.

**03 Personal and spatial practice**

Create simple meditation arrangements and considered placements for a room.

**04 Ethics and integration**

Communicate responsibly, avoid health claims, and keep practice inside an appropriate scope.

Possible takeaways
