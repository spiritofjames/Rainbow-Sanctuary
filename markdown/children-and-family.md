# Help your child explore the intelligence within.

For children ages 5–15, the Awakening Academy offers a guardian-supported space for creativity, inner awareness, intuitive discernment, and grounded self-expression.

[Explore the 4-Day Foundation](https://rainbowsanctuary.life/awakening-academy-foundation)

Live online worldwide, with selected invitation-only in-person cohorts through communities, retreats, and events.

The child’s route

## One pathway. One clear beginning.

Every new child starts with the 4-Day Foundation. Afterward, the family and team review readiness together before any later stage.

[01 Begin here **4-Day Foundation** A shared first experience.](https://rainbowsanctuary.life/awakening-academy-foundation) [02 After parent review **Level I** Awareness, discernment, expression.](https://rainbowsanctuary.life/awakening-academy-level-1) [03 Deeper practice **Level II** Care, coherence, contribution.](https://rainbowsanctuary.life/awakening-academy-level-2) [04 After completion **Certificate** Recognition of learning and participation.](https://rainbowsanctuary.life/unlock-the-potential#certificate) [05 By invitation **Ongoing community** Practice, projects, and co-creation.](https://rainbowsanctuary.life/unlock-the-potential#ongoing-practice)

**There is no performance standard.** Children participate at their own pace, with choice, care, and guardian support throughout.

What children explore

## Practice, reflection, and choice.

Rainbow Sanctuary uses spiritual language for subjective experience. These practices are invitations for attention and reflection—not medical services, diagnostic tests, or guaranteed outcomes.

### Inner screen and blindfold perception

Brief, stationary, voluntary activities with imagery, colours, or forms; a child can remove the eye mask at any time.

### Intuitive sensing

Noticing impressions around everyday objects, nature, feelings, and creative work while distinguishing observation, imagination, and uncertainty.

### Energy and boundaries

Grounding, personal space, rest, and care are practised within Rainbow Sanctuary’s spiritual framework.

### Focused intention

Later, eligible children may be invited to advanced experimental practices; these are always optional and never a measure of worth.

Family-centred and safeguard-aware

## Guardians remain part of the circle of care.

Ages 5–10 **A parent or guardian is present or immediately available.**

Ages 11–15 **A guardian consents and stays reachable during live sessions.**

Every age **Participation is voluntary; no blindfold navigation, obstacles, extended darkness, or pressure to continue.**

Rainbow Sanctuary’s children’s work is educational and experiential spiritual-wellbeing support. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace qualified medical, mental-health, or educational care.

A distinct adult pathway

## Are you an adult who wants to work with children?

The Children’s Potential Coach Certification is professional adult training in child-centred session design, family communication, safeguarding, ethics, scope, and reflective practice. It is separate from the child’s Academy route and Certificate of Accomplishment.
