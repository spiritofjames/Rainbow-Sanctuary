Planetary Symbiosis Network

Many places.
One living network.

The Planetary Symbiosis Network, or PSN, is the larger ecosystem we are building around people, projects and physical places. Its purpose is to help locally rooted communities share learning, skills and resources while caring for the Earth.

The idea is not to make every place the same. It is to respect the people, cultures and living systems that make each place unique.

Rainbow Sanctuary is one human-facing part of that vision. The School is a connected educational initiative, not another name for Rainbow or the whole network.

A few practical questions

A clearer picture,
before you begin.

Start with what matters to you. You are welcome to ask us more.

Is Rainbow Sanctuary only a retreat centre?

No. Retreats are one pathway. Rainbow also brings people together through online practice, family activities, shared learning and collaboration within a broader community vision.

Can my family join without adult retreats?

Yes. Family activities and the developing School are separate from adult retreat work. Adult participation is voluntary and never a requirement for children’s activities.

Is the School open for enrolment?

We are developing the School and preparing its digital platform for beta. Contact us about your interest; this page does not announce public enrolment or guarantee a place.

Can I visit or stay in Panama?

Please contact us first. Dates, access, accommodation and participation arrangements need to be confirmed directly. The broader vision is not a standing residency offer.

How can I support the work?

Join an appropriate activity, introduce a useful skill or project, or ask about a practical collaboration. You can also make a voluntary contribution to shared access. Contributions do not buy influence, residency or School admission.

How do I start a conversation?

Message Rainbow on Instagram, tell us what drew you here, and share the question, skill or idea you would like to explore. For programme enquiries, use the relevant programme page.
